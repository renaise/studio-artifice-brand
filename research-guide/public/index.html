About

This page lays out the R&D behind the Studio Artifice identity in the shape the final guideline will take. Everything here is a study. Nothing is yet a rule unless it says so.

Phases

Strategy, Identity, Application, Production

The question

The identity is strictly monochrome, and it carries four phase colors. What are those colors for? Three instrument traditions give the same answer: in thermal imaging, thin-film interference and color bars, color is a reading on a declared scale, never decoration.

Thesis

Color appears only with its reference, and the row of four squares is that reference. Hue never builds hierarchy. It only reports a condition.

Mark

Two shapes. The square is the printed patch. The screw is a circle with a cross cut out of its centre, and it is never drawn as a plain filled circle. Set side by side they are the device; the Ⓐ monogram sits beside the wordmark.

Color

Four phase colors, each tied to one phase. Measured, they span both media: three sit near print's process inks, one near the screen's blue primary. Mixed as light at 41 : 23 : 10 : 26 they balance to a neutral grey near L* 76, so the monochrome system is their balanced sum.

Two orders

The Figma order is canonical. Film order is a proposal: it is the order a thin film passes through as it thickens, and the order the thermal mannequin already uses for heat.

Typography

One family. Helvetica Now Display is the only typeface, everywhere: pages, decks, Rive files and the design system. There is no monospace anywhere, not even for figures or code. Hierarchy comes from scale and value, never weight; Light is kept for display sizes.

Atoms

Four primitives from the masters: square, screw, Ⓐ monogram and cross. Sorted by how much of a cell each one inks, they become a tone ramp, so any image or field can be drawn in the brand's own shapes. The chosen ramp, Brand 5, scales the cross to 72% so it sits halfway to the monogram.

The four units

The Reading

The idea every study tests. The field stays monochrome. Color appears only where a value crosses a declared threshold, and the scale it is read against is always on screen.

Color appears only with its reference, and the row of four squares is that reference.

Heat on the field

The Shape Field with the pointer as a heat source. Brightness drifts on noise and picks a glyph from the Brand 5 ramp; heat lifts cells up the ramp and cools on a known curve. Only cells past the isotherm take a phase color, banded in heat order.

Film spectrum

Thin-film color from 0 to 1000 nm, fitted as 47 stops where position equals thickness divided by ten. All four phase colors are second-order colors of one film, which gives them a physical order. A candidate brand gradient, used only where it reports a gap or a thickness.

Brand Vectorscope

Every pixel of an image plotted by its color: angle is hue, distance from the centre is saturation. Grey sits on the centre, so a monochrome page collapses to a dot. The four phase colors are target boxes. An asset on system lands in the boxes or at the centre; drift shows as a smear between them. Only pixels inside a box take its color, the isotherm rule from the thermal research.

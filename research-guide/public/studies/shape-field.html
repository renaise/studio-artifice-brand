Shape Field

Each cell reads one brightness value from a drifting noise field and draws the brand shape whose ink coverage matches it.

Glyph ramp

Numbers are measured ink coverage of the cell at the current glyph size. Brand 5 is blank, the cross (scaled so it sits halfway between blank and the monogram, size shown after @), the A monogram, the screw and the square. Extended adds scaled copies to close the gaps. Even bands gives every glyph an equal slice of the field; By coverage picks the glyph whose coverage is nearest the field value, so the image keeps its true tones.

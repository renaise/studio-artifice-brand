SAFX Color Check

Three checks built from the row of four phase colors, ported from their Rive scripts. Each one draws the same frame as its Rive artboard, takes the same inputs and reports the same view model outputs. Colors, label size, spacing and the ease are the brand library's token defaults.

What it is

A reference sent before the work. Leader builds the four bars in, fills the slate, holds with a countdown and an optional 1 kHz tone, then cuts to ink. Standby is the site's no-signal state: bars hold, the slate shows a status and a clock runs. Hover a bar to read it.

Drag to heat · hold still to heat longer

Reading it. The pointer is a heat source. Glyphs show heat on the Brand 5 ramp and fade as the field cools. Where heat stays above the threshold, an oxide grows and keeps its colour after cooling, so the field records where work was done. Thin film reads blue, then cyan, yellow-green and magenta as it thickens. In Figma order the same four positions take the Figma colours.

Logo sting · the mark comes into register

Reading it. The circle is an optical flat over the mark. Its fringes map the gap to the master, one fringe per half wavelength. As the gap closes the rings widen and thin, and at contact the circle goes black. The square arrives as two misregistered layers whose seam carries film colour until they align. The plus knocks out last, at contact.
